# Skyline Global Industries — Visual Redesign Plan

## Direction
Premium international glove manufacturer. Editorial product catalogue + industrial manufacturing credibility. Deep navy is 25% of the visual system; white/light neutral remains dominant; Skyline blue/cyan are accents.

## Priorities
1. Brand system: typography, spacing, navy/white/blue palette, buttons, navigation, section rhythm.
2. Hero: one dominant premium glove composition; remove cluttered multi-object/globe-heavy treatment.
3. About: catalogue-inspired editorial layout with image + four proof points.
4. Products: photography-first digital catalogue for all 55 references and six categories.
5. OEM: dark campaign-style section using black/white glove imagery.
6. Manufacturing: sequential factory story.
7. Quality/Credentials: distinguish corporate registration from product quality; no defensive certificate language.
8. Gallery: visual product archive.
9. Animation: restrained, purposeful reveals/hover motion.
10. Responsive polish.

## Design rules
- Do not use black as the default site background.
- Do not overuse circular globe graphics.
- Do not make every section a rounded card grid.
- Product photography is the visual hero.
- Large typography is used selectively for editorial hierarchy.
- Deep navy target is approximately 25% of page visual area, concentrated in hero/OEM/navigation/footer and selected bands.
- Avoid generic SaaS dashboard aesthetics.
- Keep real Cloudinary product imagery and SKU/category data.
